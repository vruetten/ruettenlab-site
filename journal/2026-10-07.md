# 2026-10-07

The site moved to https://www.wholisticlab.org, the koi water went live on the home page, News
gained a second weight of item, and the slow pages were made faster. `main` ends the session at
the commit that adds this entry; `water-shimmer` matches it.

## What changed

- **News content.** Added the Nature Neuroscience research highlight "WHOLISTIC tracks the
  entire body" (William P. Olson, 2026-10-01), the Zeminar online talk (2026-10-01), the talk at
  Janelia's "No Brainer" conference (October 2026) and the talk at The Body in Dialogue, Paris
  Brain Institute (December 2026). Talk dates and links came from Virginie's mail and the hosts'
  pages; the two upcoming talks show the month only, because the day of the Janelia talk is not
  public and the Paris day was "tentatively" 5 December.
- **News layout.** Items without an image render as brief tiles (host logo, date, title) in the
  same grid as the photo cards, two or three to a cell; the separate "Lab" section is gone. The
  page orders by date alone; the `lead`, `pin` and `rank` fields were removed. Host logos are
  stored in `src/assets/news/logos/` and drawn in the text colour. The home page lists ten items
  instead of five.
- **Princeton photograph.** A new photograph replaced the Princeton news card image and became
  the Join page backdrop at 16% opacity, painted through the sticky navigation bar as well. The
  14 MB original is in the git-ignored `originals/` folder.
- **Tab icon.** The koi image replaced Astro's default favicon (`favicon.ico` 16/32/48 px,
  `icon-192.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`).
- **Water.** A background agent replaced the star-drawn word WHOLISTIC with the koi icon
  (`src/assets/koi-whirl.png`): two koi as yin and yang around a spiral with four nodes, the
  stars slowly circling it. The water now runs at every width and drops below the news list
  under 960 px. It went live on `main` once `scripts/check-weight.mjs` was changed to let the
  home page ship JavaScript.
- **Domain.** `astro.config.mjs` moved to `site: 'https://www.wholisticlab.org'` and `base: '/'`.
  The Pages custom domain is `www.wholisticlab.org`, HTTPS enforced. DNS records were added in
  Squarespace by a separate agent; the domain was verified on the GitHub account. Details are in
  `../CLAUDE.md`.
- **Speed.** The News page no longer hides itself until every photo loads; photos below the
  first row lazy-load; photo widths are capped at 1000 px at quality 72; the Join backdrop went
  from 514 KB to 92 KB (1280 px, light blur).
- **Notes.** Added `../CLAUDE.md` (hosting, DNS, paths, conventions) and this journal. Domain
  ownership and the wholistic.* purchase inquiries are recorded in the private
  `~/Documents/princeton/lab_admin/working/web-domains.md`.

## What was measured

- The account `vruetten` is on GitHub Free (read from the billing page), so the repository must
  stay public for Pages.
- After the switch, `http://wholisticlab.org` and `https://vruetten.github.io/ruettenlab-site/news/`
  answered 301 to `www.wholisticlab.org` with the path kept, and all 62 internal links of the
  root-base build returned 200 from a local server (curl, 2026-10-07).
- Page speed, live site, headless Chromium on this Mac in Ashburn, median of 5 cold loads per
  page. "Slow 4G phone" is simulated: 150 ms round trip, 1.6 Mbit/s down, CPU slowed four times,
  390 px viewport at three device pixels per CSS pixel.

  | Page | Phone: first paint before → after | Phone: transferred before → after |
  |---|---|---|
  | News | 5996 ms → 592 ms | 1063 KB → 364 KB |
  | Join | 776 ms → 776 ms (load 3389 → 1512 ms) | 590 KB → 180 KB |
  | Home | 736 ms → 676 ms (not changed by the fixes) | 299 KB → 299 KB |

  On the unthrottled connection every page painted within about 0.25–0.6 s both before and
  after. Server first byte by curl was about 50 ms (mean of 5 per page), served from GitHub's
  cache in the Washington, D.C. area; in-browser first-byte figures varied from 69 to 353 ms,
  most likely from cache misses caused by the random query string the test added (assumed,
  not checked).
- Home page weight after the water went live: 20.7 KB JavaScript, 14.4 KB CSS (`check-weight`).

Not measured: the water's frame rate on real hardware (headless Chromium renders WebGL in
software), and any visitor far from the Washington, D.C. cache.

## What was wrong

- The local server was opened at a page width under 960 px (a 1080-point window at 125% zoom),
  so the water panel was hidden, and Virginie reported that the shimmer did not show.
- Running a second local server on port 4322 for the news branch left the water and the news
  on different ports, and Virginie looked for the fish on the wrong one. The branches were
  merged so one port showed both.
- The first brief-tile layout stretched its grid row to 475 px (measured) and coloured every
  tile orange; the tiles were redone as slim rows in the text colour (326 px for three).
- A Chrome tab opened by the automation tools landed in Virginie's own window, where it stayed
  hidden and the canvas did not animate; checks moved to headless Chromium.
- Right after the domain switch, Virginie saw the page as unstyled text. The likely cause
  (inferred, not observed) is that the custom domain briefly served the old build, whose files
  sat under `/ruettenlab-site/`, before the new deploy finished; a check minutes later found the
  new build and its stylesheet loading.
